### Your Conflict Resolution Framework

1. **De-escalate & Empathize (Take Ease & Sit Together):**
    
      
    - Recognize that most conflicts stem from simple misunderstandings—miscommunications between team members or unclear instructions from a Product Owner (PO).
        
          
        
    - Bring the parties together in a calm, neutral environment to talk openly and clear the air.
        
          
        
2. **Isolate & Extract Key Points:**
    
      
    - Listen actively to all sides without judgment.
        
          
        
    - Distill the emotional noise to identify the core technical, process, or alignment bottlenecks causing the friction.
        
          
        
3. **Collaborative Action Planning:**
    
      
    - Work _together_ with the team members to co-create a clear, actionable remediation plan. Ownership of the solution prevents future resentment.
        
          
        
4. **Execute & Follow Through:**
    
      
    - Put the plan into action and maintain active visibility on the situation until it is fully resolved and normal team rhythm is restored.
        
          
        
5. **Leveraging HR Strategically:**
    
      
    - Recognize when a situation requires specialized intervention. Involve HR representatives when interpersonal or "non-talk" blockages run deep, utilizing their expertise to refine the action plan and restore healthy communication.
